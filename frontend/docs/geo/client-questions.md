# Questions for the client (GEO G2–G3)

Answers to these unlock the remaining answerable content. Nothing below is published until the client confirms it. Where a question is about Ethiopian rules (visas, health, permits), the site will state what the client confirms and link to the official source, with a review date.

## Decisions

1. **AI training crawlers** (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot): allow or block? Blocking keeps the site's text out of model training; the site stays visible in AI search either way. Currently allowed.

## Practical facts travellers ask about

2. **Visas**: may the site say which nationalities can get a visa on arrival at Bole, or should it only link to evisa.gov.et? Do you help with e-visa applications?
3. **Payment**: which payment methods do you accept for the deposit and balance (card, PayPal, bank transfer, cash)? The footer shows Visa, Mastercard and PayPal.
4. **Cancellation**: a one-paragraph plain-language summary of the cancellation terms you are happy to show outside the full terms.
5. **Guides**: which languages can you provide on request (Spanish, French, Italian, German…)?
6. **Group tours**: do you run fixed-date group departures, or are "group" tours groups the traveller forms? (SafariBookings says "Customizable private tours and fixed group tours"; if fixed departures exist, which tours and dates?)
7. **Children and families**: minimum ages or restrictions for any tours (Danakil, Simien treks)?
8. **Accessibility**: which tours suit travellers with limited mobility?
9. **Health and altitude**: what advice do you give about altitude (Simien, Bale, Lalibela) and vaccinations? The site would link to official health guidance.
10. **Safety**: how do you describe current travel conditions by region, and which official advisories do you follow?
11. **Getting there**: for each main destination, nearest airport and typical road time (for the destination pages' "how to get there").
12. **Tipping**: what tipping guidance do you give guests?
13. **Accommodation**: standard hotel level across tours, and whether guests can upgrade.

## Off-site presence (G3)

14. Do you own the Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and Tripadvisor accounts linked in the new site's footer? Facebook and Tripadvisor are also linked from the old site; Instagram and YouTube are not. Please confirm or share the correct URLs.
15. Is there a Google Business Profile for the Lalibela office? Who manages it?
16. Memberships, licences or associations that can be named and linked. SafariBookings lists ETOA, ETGA and the Lalibela & Surrounding Guide association: are these current, and do they have public member pages?
17. **Founding year**: SafariBookings says 2019, SafariGo says 2011. Which is right? Both profiles should then be corrected.
18. **Addis Ababa office**: SafariGo says "Lalibela and Addis Ababa based". Is there an Addis Ababa office, and if so, its address?
